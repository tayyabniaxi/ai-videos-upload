Privacy Policy – AI Videos Upload

Last updated: 5 November 2026

AI Videos Upload is a personal tool run on the owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What data the app uses

  • With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API scope to upload videos and set video details (title, description, tags, privacy status, scheduled publish time).
  • The OAuth access/refresh token is stored only on the owner's computer. It is never sold, shared or sent to any third party.
  • The app does not collect, store or share any data about other people or viewers.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
